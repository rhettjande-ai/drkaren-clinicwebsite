About

A practice built for people far from their own doctor.

Dr. Karen Mozo is a Physician and Surgeon who graduated from the Universidad Nacional San Antonio Abad del Cusco, holding a Master's degree in Epidemiological Research from Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia and a Master's degree in Health Policy and Management from Universidad Nacional San Antonio Abad del Cusco. She is currently a Doctoral candidate at Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia and has worked in research related to travel medicine for the past 14 years, alongside a private clinical practice in travel medicine since the beginning of her career. Her extensive experience in this field ensures the quality of care she provides.
